---
name: Tidy email design system
description: Required visual branding shared by every customer, Pro, applicant, owner/admin, alert, digest, and account email
type: design
---

Every outgoing email, regardless of recipient or trigger, must look intentionally designed and unmistakably Tidy.

- Use the legitimate Tidy logo from a stable public jointidy.co asset.
- Match the established customer-email aesthetic: navy header, gold accent, small white banner with service icons, clean white content cards, thoughtful spacing, and a polished legal footer.
- Preserve readable mobile formatting and email-client-safe table/inline styling.
- Internal/admin alerts are not exempt; they use the same shell, with denser information inside it when needed.
- Live provider templates and code-generated emails must remain visually consistent.
- Preserve each email's business meaning, recipient, trigger, template ID, and merge fields while standardizing presentation.
