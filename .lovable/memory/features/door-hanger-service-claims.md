---
name: Door-hanger service claims
description: Product requirements behind the printed 48-hour, photo-verified, one-bill, and Pro-consistency claims
type: feature
---

- Terms must say: “Notify us within 48 hours of any service issue.” Terms cannot be stricter than the public 48-hour guarantee.
- Before-and-after photos are required before every visit can be completed, and members must see those real photos on that visit’s record.
- A multi-service membership must produce one combined recurring charge and one bill, never a separate subscription or charge per service.
- Cleaning, lawn, and car care may have different specialists. Never imply one Pro handles all services. Use “the same pro for each service, every visit.” Spanish: “el mismo profesional para cada servicio, en cada visita.”
