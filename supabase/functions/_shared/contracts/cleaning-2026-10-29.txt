1. WHAT THIS AGREEMENT COVERS

1.1 Tidy Home Concierge LLC ("Tidy") runs a membership service that connects homeowners in Miami-Dade County ("Members") with independent service professionals. Contractor runs an independent house cleaning business.

1.2 Under this agreement, Contractor performs residential house cleaning visits and the cleaning add-ons listed in Schedule A (the "Services") for Members booked through Tidy. This agreement is not exclusive: Contractor is free to run its own business and work for anyone else, subject only to Section 12.



2. CONTRACTOR RUNS ITS OWN BUSINESS

2.1 Contractor is an independent contractor. This agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture or agency, and Contractor cannot sign anything or make promises on Tidy's behalf.

2.2 Contractor decides how to do the work — methods, order, techniques and pace — to deliver the results described in Schedule B.

2.3 Contractor supplies and pays for its own equipment, supplies, vehicle and phone, including its vacuum, mop and bucket, microfiber cloths, scrub brushes, a step stool, and household-safe cleaning products.

2.4 Contractor sets its own availability and may accept or decline visits before they are committed. Contractor is not entitled to wages, overtime, benefits, unemployment pay or workers' compensation from Tidy.



3. AVAILABILITY AND SCHEDULING

3.1 Contractor enters the days, hours and ZIP codes it wants to work in the Tidy Pro app. Visits Tidy books inside that availability are committed visits.

3.2 Cleaning visits are booked into three arrival windows: 8–10 AM, 11 AM–1 PM and 2–4 PM, one home per window. Contractor arrives inside the window shown in the Pro app.

3.3 Rain does not change cleaning visits; they go ahead as scheduled.

3.4 Availability changes apply to visits not yet booked. Before removing a day that already has booked visits, Contractor gives at least 14 days' notice in the Pro app.

3.5 If Contractor cannot make a committed visit, Contractor tells Tidy through the Pro app or at (786) 829-1141 as early as possible and at least 24 hours ahead, except for real emergencies. Repeated missed visits are a reason to end this agreement.

3.6 Cleaning sizes are set by the home's square footage and checked against Miami-Dade Property Appraiser records. If a home is clearly larger than the size it was booked at, Contractor reports it in the Pro app. Tidy reviews it and moves the Member to the correct size; Contractor is paid the corrected rate starting with the visit Tidy confirms.



4. PAY

4.1 Tidy pays Contractor a flat rate for each completed visit, as listed in Schedule A. The rate is the same whether the Member is on a monthly, every-two-weeks or weekly plan.

4.2 Add-ons pay 40% of the add-on's Member price (Schedule A), including add-ons Tidy gives Members free as a gift.

4.3 A visit is complete when Contractor checks out in the Pro app with the required photos.

4.4 Payday is every Friday, by direct deposit through Stripe, for visits completed in the previous pay week as shown in the Pro app. Contractor sets up its Stripe payout account in the Pro app before its first visit.

4.5 Review bonus — $25. Every five-star Google review from a member Contractor served that names Contractor pays $25, added to that Friday's deposit. One per member.

4.6 Tier 2 Pro Partner — +10% on all visit and add-on pay, once Contractor has completed 50+ visits, holds a 4.8+ rating and has worked with Tidy 60+ days.

4.7 Insurance help — $50 a month for Contractor's first 3 months, added to Friday pay, once a valid certificate is on file.

4.8 Late cancellations and lockouts: when a Member cancels with less than 24 hours' notice, or Contractor is locked out as described in this agreement, Tidy pays Contractor 50% of the visit rate.

4.9 48-hour redo: if a Member reports a problem and Contractor returns within 48 hours to fix it, Tidy pays Contractor 50% of the visit rate for the return trip.

4.10 Contractor raises any pay question within 14 days of the Friday it concerns. Tidy may change rates with at least 30 days' written notice; changes are never retroactive, and Contractor may end this agreement instead of accepting them.

4.11 There are no other bonuses, stipends or reimbursements unless they are written into this agreement or an amendment signed by Tidy.



5. TAXES

5.1 Tidy does not withhold any taxes. Contractor is responsible for all of its own federal, state and local taxes and filings.

5.2 Contractor provides its taxpayer information (SSN or EIN) securely through the Stripe payout setup in the Pro app. Tidy does not collect Social Security numbers on paper. Tidy issues a Form 1099 each January as the law requires.



6. INSURANCE

6.1 Before its first paid visit, and for as long as this agreement lasts, Contractor keeps a commercial general liability policy of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, naming Tidy Home Concierge LLC, 2121 Biscayne Blvd #1562, Miami, FL 33137 as additional insured.

6.2 Contractor uploads its certificate of insurance in the Pro app. If the policy lapses or expires, new visits pause until a current certificate is on file.

6.3 Contractor keeps the auto insurance Florida law requires for its vehicle, and any workers' compensation coverage Florida law requires if Contractor has employees or helpers.



7. BACKGROUND CHECK

7.1 Before its first paid visit, Contractor completes a criminal and motor vehicle background check through a consumer reporting agency, at Tidy's cost. Contractor signs a separate disclosure and authorization form for it.

7.2 Members let Contractor into their homes, so a check Tidy cannot clear means no visits can be assigned.



8. HELPERS AND SUBSTITUTES

8.1 Contractor may bring a helper or send a substitute only if that person has been approved by Tidy in advance, has passed Tidy's background check, and is covered by Contractor's insurance.

8.2 Contractor pays its own helpers and is fully responsible for their work and conduct in Members' homes.



9. STANDARDS AND CONDUCT

9.1 Each visit delivers the results in Schedule B. Contractor checks in and out in the Pro app; the app records location at check-in and check-out only, plus before-and-after photos of the work.

9.2 Tidy guarantees Members a fix within 48 hours. When a Member reports a problem, Contractor returns within 48 hours when it can; if Contractor cannot, Tidy may send another Pro.

9.3 In Members' homes and yards, Contractor brings no guests, does not smoke, drink alcohol or use drugs, does not use Member property beyond what the work needs, and takes no photos except the required work photos.

9.4 Contractor reports any damage, injury or safety issue to Tidy the same day.

9.5 Lockouts. If Contractor arrives inside the window and cannot get in, Contractor waits 15 minutes, contacts Tidy through the Pro app or at (786) 829-1141, and checks out with a photo of the door. Tidy pays Contractor 50% of the visit rate for a verified lockout.

9.6 Estate Plus homes (two-Pro team). Estate Plus homes (4,800–7,000 sq ft) are always cleaned by a two-Pro team. The team visit pays $238: $119 to each Pro, or $238 to the owner when the team is an owner plus an approved helper from the same business. Add-ons are paid to the Pro who performs them.

9.7 Keys, codes and alarms. Contractor uses Member keys, gate codes, lockbox codes and alarm codes only for the visit, never copies a key, and reports a lost key or a code problem to Tidy right away.

9.8 Pets. Contractor keeps doors and gates closed so pets stay inside, follows the pet notes in the Pro app, and tells Tidy before the visit if Contractor cannot work around a pet.



10. ITEMS TIDY PROVIDES

10.1 Tidy provides Contractor, at no cost, a uniform kit and a photo ID badge. Contractor may wear them on Tidy visits; the badge lets Members confirm who is at their door.

10.2 The badge stays Tidy property and is returned when this agreement ends. Vehicle magnets are optional and covered by a separate vehicle advertising agreement.



11. CONFIDENTIALITY AND MEMBER SAFETY

11.1 Member names, addresses, phone numbers, schedules, keys and access, gate, lockbox and alarm codes are confidential. Contractor uses them only to perform the Services, never shares them, and deletes or returns them when this agreement ends.



12. MEMBERS STAY WITH TIDY

12.1 While this agreement lasts and for 12 months after it ends, Contractor will not solicit or accept direct work for house cleaning, lawn care or car care from any Tidy Member Contractor met through Tidy, and will not encourage a Member to cancel with Tidy. Contractor remains free to work for anyone else.



13. ENDING THE AGREEMENT

13.1 Either party may end this agreement at any time by written notice, including text or email. Contractor is asked to give 14 days' notice so committed visits are covered.

13.2 Tidy may end this agreement immediately for theft, a safety concern, harassment, damage caused by gross carelessness, an insurance lapse, a background check Tidy cannot clear, soliciting Members, alcohol or drug use on a visit, or false check-ins or photos.

13.3 Tidy pays for all completed visits through the final Friday. Contractor returns the badge and any Member keys, and deletes Member codes and information.



14. RESPONSIBILITY FOR CLAIMS

14.1 Contractor is responsible for, and will defend and cover Tidy against, claims caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Contractor or its helpers. Tidy is responsible for, and will defend and cover Contractor against, claims caused by Tidy's own negligence or willful misconduct.



15. DISAGREEMENTS

15.1 If a disagreement comes up, both parties first try to work it out directly for at least 15 days. Florida law governs this agreement, and any case is brought in the courts of Miami-Dade County, Florida.



16. GENERAL

16.1 This agreement, with Schedules A and B, is the full agreement between the parties and replaces any earlier promises. Changes must be in writing and signed (electronically is fine) by both parties.

16.2 If any part of this agreement is found unenforceable, the rest still applies. Electronic signatures count as originals. Notices go to hello@jointidy.co and to the email or phone Contractor keeps on file in the Pro app.

SCHEDULE B — THE TIDY STANDARD: HOUSE CLEANING

The results every home should show at check-out. How Contractor gets there is Contractor's call.

Kitchen: Counters and backsplash wiped; sink and faucet cleaned and shined; stovetop and outside of appliances wiped; cabinet fronts spot-cleaned; trash emptied; floor vacuumed and mopped.

Bathrooms: Toilet cleaned inside and out; tub, shower and glass cleaned; sink, counter and faucet cleaned; mirrors streak-free; trash emptied; floor vacuumed and mopped.

Bedrooms and living areas: Reachable surfaces dusted; beds made with the sheets already on them (a sheet change is the Linen Change add-on); mirrors and glass tables cleaned; floors vacuumed and mopped; baseboards spot-cleaned.

Entryways: Inside of the front-door glass cleaned; entry floor vacuumed and mopped; mat straightened.

Finish: Lights off and doors locked as the Member asked; before-and-after photos and check-out done in the Pro app.
