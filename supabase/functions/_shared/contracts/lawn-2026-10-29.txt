WHAT THIS AGREEMENT COVERS 1.1 Tidy Home Concierge LLC ("Tidy") runs a membership service that connects homeowners in Miami-Dade County ("Members") with independent service professionals. Contractor runs an independent lawn care business. 1.2 Under this agreement, Contractor performs residential lawn care visits and the lawn add-ons listed in Schedule A (the "Services") for Members booked through Tidy. This agreement is not exclusive: Contractor is free to run its own business and work for anyone else, subject only to Section 12.  2. CONTRACTOR RUNS ITS OWN BUSINESS 2.1 Contractor is an independent contractor. This agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture or agency, and Contractor cannot sign anything or make promises on Tidy's behalf. 2.2 Contractor decides how to do the work — methods, order, techniques and pace — to deliver the results described in Schedule B. 2.3 Contractor supplies and pays for its own equipment, supplies, vehicle and phone, including its mower, string trimmer, edger, blower, hand tools, yard-waste bags, safety gear, and a vehicle (and trailer if needed) that can carry it all. 2.4 Contractor sets its own availability and may accept or decline visits before they are committed. Contractor is not entitled to wages, overtime, benefits, unemployment pay or workers' compensation from Tidy.  3. AVAILABILITY AND SCHEDULING 3.1 Contractor enters the days, hours and ZIP codes it wants to work in the Tidy Pro app. Visits Tidy books inside that availability are committed visits. 3.2 Lawn visit days are assigned by street so each route stays tight. Contractor completes each yard on its scheduled day. 3.3 Rain-outs: Contractor decides when conditions are unsafe or too wet to cut. A rained-out visit moves to the Friday catch-up day or the next open day. Contractor may book its own other work on Fridays; Tidy gives at least 24 hours' notice when a Friday is needed for rain-outs. Visits that are not performed are not paid. 3.4 Availability changes apply to visits not yet booked. Before removing a day that already has booked visits, Contractor gives at least 14 days' notice in the Pro app. 3.5 If Contractor cannot make a committed visit, Contractor tells Tidy through the Pro app or at (786) 829-1141 as early as possible and at least 24 hours ahead, except for real emergencies. Repeated missed visits are a reason to end this agreement. 3.6 Lawn sizes are measured in square feet of turf only (not the whole lot) and confirmed from aerial imagery. If a yard is clearly larger than the size it was booked at, Contractor reports it in the Pro app. Tidy reviews it and moves the Member to the correct size; Contractor is paid the corrected rate starting with the visit Tidy confirms.  4. PAY 4.1 Tidy pays Contractor a flat rate for each completed visit, as listed in Schedule A. The rate is the same whether the Member is on a monthly, every-two-weeks or weekly plan. 4.2 Add-ons pay 40% of the add-on's Member price (Schedule A), including add-ons Tidy gives Members free as a gift. 4.3 A visit is complete when Contractor checks out in the Pro app with the required photos. 4.4 Payday is every Friday, by direct deposit through Stripe, for visits completed in the previous pay week as shown in the Pro app. Contractor sets up its Stripe payout account in the Pro app before its first visit. 4.5 Review bonus — $25. Every five-star Google review from a member Contractor served that names Contractor pays $25, added to that Friday's deposit. One per member. 4.6 Tier 2 Pro Partner — +10% on all visit and add-on pay, once Contractor has completed 50+ visits, holds a 4.8+ rating and has worked with Tidy 60+ days. 4.7 Insurance help — $50 a month for Contractor's first 3 months, added to Friday pay, once a valid certificate is on file. 4.8 Late cancellations and lockouts: when a Member cancels with less than 24 hours' notice, or Contractor is locked out as described in this agreement, Tidy pays Contractor 50% of the visit rate. 4.9 48-hour redo: if a Member reports a problem and Contractor returns within 48 hours to fix it, Tidy pays Contractor 50% of the visit rate for the return trip. 4.10 Contractor raises any pay question within 14 days of the Friday it concerns. Tidy may change rates with at least 30 days' written notice; changes are never retroactive, and Contractor may end this agreement instead of accepting them. 4.11 There are no other bonuses, stipends or reimbursements unless they are written into this agreement or an amendment signed by Tidy.  5. STANDARDS AND CONDUCT 9.1 Each visit delivers the results in Schedule B. Contractor checks in and out in the Pro app; the app records location at check-in and check-out only, plus before-and-after photos of the work. 9.2 Tidy guarantees Members a fix within 48 hours.
